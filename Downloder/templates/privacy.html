We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, security, troubleshooting and legal obligations. Actual retention depends on the server and hosting configuration.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Vistan is not intended to encourage children to submit personal or sensitive information. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact the site owner so the matter can be reviewed.
